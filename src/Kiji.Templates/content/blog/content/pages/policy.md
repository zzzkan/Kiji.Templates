## Privacy

This site serves static pages. It does not use analytics, advertising, cookies,
or contact forms. The hosting provider may keep access logs, such as IP addresses
and request times, to operate the service.

Syntax highlighting scripts, language definitions, and styles are loaded from
jsDelivr. Your browser contacts that CDN when you visit a page.

## External links

Some posts link to other websites. Those sites have their own content and privacy
policies.

## Reusing content

Unless a page says otherwise, the writing and original images belong to the site
author. Please link to the original page when sharing a post and ask the author
before republishing it. Third-party software keeps its own license.
